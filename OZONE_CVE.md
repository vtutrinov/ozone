### Jars built into the Ozone distribution

| CVE | Jar | Exploitable in Ozone? | Action |

The bug leaks old output-buffer contents on crafted input. No remote input reaches the decompressor. | **Fixed:** `aircompressor.version=2.0.3` in root dependencyManagement.
